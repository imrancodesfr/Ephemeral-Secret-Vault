How it works

Guardian-Held Shares

The server keeps only one-way commitments to each share — guardians hold the real shares and reveal them only during recovery.

Shamir Secret Sharing

Encrypted secrets split into shares; a threshold of k-of-n shares reconstructs the secret.

Audit Trail

Every action — create, renew, expire, recover — is appended to a proof-of-work ledger that detects tampering.

Dead Man's Switch

Failure to renew triggers recovery mode automatically after expiry.

Guardian Governance

Recovery requires a threshold of guardian-held shares — no single holder, not even the server, can unlock the secret alone.

Document Notary

SHA-256 fingerprints notarized in the ledger confirm a document is unmodified over time.
