Document Notary

Register a cryptographic fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) of a document on the local blockchain, then verify that a copy is unmodified. The document content itself never goes on-chain.

This demonstration hashes documents locally and stores the hash in both the SQLite database and local blockchain transactions. Keep the content yourself — only the fingerprint is notarized.
