Secret Recovery Vault

Recover sensitive information through threshold cryptography. Guardians submit their individual shares to reconstruct the secret when the dead man's switch triggers.

One-Time Ephemeral Delivery: Once recovered, the secret is shown exactly once. The server permanently wipes the ciphertext, wrapped key, and guardian share copies from the database. A recovered vault can never be recovered again.

How Keys & Shares Work in Recovery

1. What is a Guardian Share?

When a vault is created, the AES key is split into polynomial points. Each guardian receives one share: {"x": 1, "y": [...]}. The server stores only its SHA-256 hash commitment.

2. Who Can Activate?

If a vault's timer runs out (owner unresponsive), the Recovery Recipient or an authorized Guardian activates recovery mode. The owner can also activate it manually for emergency access.

3. How to Submit?

Guardians click "Pull share from notifications" or paste their {"x": ..., "y": [...]} JSON. Once k valid shares arrive, the recipient reconstructs the secret.

Recovery Workflow

1 · Activate Recovery (Dead Man's Switch)

Owners and designated recipients may start recovery once a vault has expired. If the owner has failed to renew, this triggers the emergency recovery pipeline and alerts all guardians.

2 · Submit Your Guardian Share

If you are a designated guardian for an active recovery, submit your unique mathematical shard (x, y). The server compares its SHA-256 hash against the pre-recorded zero-knowledge commitment.
