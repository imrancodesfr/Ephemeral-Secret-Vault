🛡 What is a Guardian?

A Guardian holds exactly 1 mathematical shard of your secret. A single guardian cannot read or steal your secret alone. You need at least 2 guardians. Each selected user will hold one share.

4 · Threshold to reconstruct

Shamir Secret Sharing uses a k-of-n threshold. Any k of the n guardians can reconstruct the secret, even if some guardians lose their share.

🎯 What is a Recovery Recipient?

The Recipient is the designated beneficiary. When the dead man's switch expires, the recipient is the ONLY entity who receives and views the reconstructed secret once the required guardian shares are submitted.
